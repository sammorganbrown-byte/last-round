Put it on your phone

It’s not in the App Store. It lives on the web and installs to your home screen in a few seconds.

  1. Tap Open Last Round above. It must open in Safari (if you’re in another app, use “Open in Safari”).
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it from the new icon on your home screen.
  4. Tap Create account with your email and a password. You’re straight in, no confirmation email needed.

How to use it

  • Tap your drink on the coaster. Your four most-used drinks sit right on it, each showing exactly how many standard drinks it is. The bars fill up, one per standard drink.
  • Something else? Tap the coaster anywhere else for every drink, size and your own custom ones.
  • Got it wrong? Hit Undo, or tap the drink to change it.
  • The big number is what’s left of your 10 this week. Weeks run Monday to Sunday and start fresh every Monday.
  • Tap a day along the top to add something you forgot, or swap a night off.
  • Your stopping point: tell it when nights usually slide, and it nudges you to stop one drink before.

The only real rule: don’t lie to it.

It’s not your parents. Go over and nobody grounds you. But log it honestly, so the rest of the week can adjust.
